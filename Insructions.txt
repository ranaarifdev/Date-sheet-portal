📢 Mid-Term Examination Guidelines

Dear Students,

All students are instructed to strictly follow these guidelines during the Mid-Term Examinations:

1. Follow the approved date sheet and arrive at the examination room at least 15 minutes before the scheduled time.

2. Bring your valid university ID card.

3. Late arrival will be dealt with according to university rules.

4. Bring your own stationery. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and other unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited.

5. Maintain silence and discipline throughout the examination.

6. Cheating, copying, impersonation, and the use of unauthorized material are strictly prohibited.

7. Write your name, roll number, and other required information correctly on the answer sheet.

8. Do not leave the examination hall without the invigilator’s permission.

9. Follow all instructions issued by the invigilation staff and the Incharge Examination.

⚠️ Important: Any violation of these instructions may result in disciplinary action under university rules.

Your cooperation is expected to ensure the smooth and fair conduct of examinations.

Incharge Examination
Faculty of Computing & Emerging Technologies

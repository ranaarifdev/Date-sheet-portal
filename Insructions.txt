# FACULTY OF COMPUTING & EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES
## MID-TERM EXAMINATION – IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS



All students are hereby informed that the Mid-Term Examinations will commence on Monday, 02 November 2026. Students are strictly advised to follow the instructions below to ensure the smooth, fair, and disciplined conduct of examinations.

### 1. General Examination Instructions

1. Follow the approved date sheet and arrive at the examination room at least **15 minutes before** the scheduled examination time.
2. Bring your valid university ID card.
3. Late arrival will be dealt with according to university rules.
4. Bring your own stationery. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and other unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited.
5. Maintain silence, discipline, and proper conduct throughout the examination.
6. Cheating, copying, impersonation, and the use of unauthorized materials are strictly prohibited.
7. Write your name, roll number, and all other required information correctly on the answer sheet.
8. Do not leave the examination hall without the invigilator's permission.
9. Follow all instructions issued by the invigilation staff and the Incharge Examination.

### 2. Examination Fee and Dues Clearance

1. All students who have outstanding semester dues must clear their pending fees before the examinations.
2. Students whose names appear on the fee-defaulter list must bring their **paid semester fee voucher** as proof of payment.
3. Students who have already paid their dues but whose names still appear on the defaulter list must present their paid fee voucher at the Admin Office for verification.
4. Students with unpaid semester dues will not be permitted to sit in the examinations.

### 3. Instructions for Repeater Students

1. All repeater students must bring their **paid supplementary examination fee voucher of Rs. 6,025/-** during the examinations.
2. No repeater student will be allowed to appear in the examination without proof of payment of the supplementary examination fee.
3. Repeater students who have any clash between regular and supplementary examination papers must immediately report to the Admin Office (Mr. Irfan Ajmal) for resolution before the examination.
4. Students who have applied for repeat subjects but are facing any errors or discrepancies in their fee vouchers must immediately visit the Admin Office for correction.
5. Any claimed examination clash must be genuine and verified by the Admin Office. False claims regarding examination clashes may result in cancellation of the concerned paper and further action under university rules.

### 4. Classroom Discipline and Examination Arrangements

1. Enter only the examination room assigned to you according to the seating arrangement.
2. Do not change, disturb, or rearrange the assigned seating arrangements. Chairs have been arranged according to examination SOPs.
3. Do not leave question papers behind after completing your examination.
4. Do not throw wrappers, papers, or other litter in the classrooms. Keep the examination rooms clean.
5. Cooperate with the examination staff, particularly during the transition between examination slots.
6. As there will be limited time between examination slots for classroom cleaning and preparation, all students must leave their rooms in a clean and orderly condition.

### 5. Important Reminder

All students are advised to clear their semester dues and resolve any fee voucher or examination schedule discrepancies before the examinations to avoid inconvenience on examination days.

**Warning:** Any violation of these instructions may result in disciplinary action in accordance with university rules. Students who fail to meet the examination fee requirements will not be allowed to sit in the examinations.

Your cooperation is essential for maintaining a fair, transparent, and well-organized examination environment.

**Best wishes for your examinations!**

**Incharge Examination**
